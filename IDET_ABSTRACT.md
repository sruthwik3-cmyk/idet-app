# IDET Project Abstract

**Project Title:** IDET - Important Documents Expiry Tracker

**Abstract:**

Managing essential personal and professional documents—such as passports, insurance policies, driving licenses, and vehicle registrations—is a critical but often disorganized task for many individuals. The failure to renew these documents on time can result in significant financial penalties, legal complications, and service disruptions. Existing methods, ranging from physical filing systems to manual calendar reminders, are prone to human error and lack centralized accessibility.

This project introduces **IDET (Important Documents Expiry Tracker)**, a modern, intelligent web application designed to streamline document management and automate expiry tracking. The system serves as a secure, centralized repository where users can store document details and receive proactive alerts before critical deadlines.

**Key capabilities of the system include:**
*   **Smart Notification Engine:** Automates alerts through multiple channels, integrating directly with **Gmail** for email reminders and **Google Calendar** for schedule management, ensuring users are notified well in advance of any expiry.
*   **Real-Time Dashboard:** Provides a clear, visual analytics overview of total documents, creating an immediate understanding of active versus expiring status.
*   **Cross-Device Synchronization:** utilizing a cloud-based architecture (Supabase), the application ensures that data is instantly updated and accessible across all devices, from desktops to mobile phones.
*   **User-Centric Design:** Features a responsive interface with specialized mobile views and an interactive calendar to visualize upcoming expirations effortlessly.

By automating the monitoring process and providing a unified view of all important documentation, IDET significantly reduces the administrative burden on users, offering peace of mind and preventing the consequences of missed renewals.
