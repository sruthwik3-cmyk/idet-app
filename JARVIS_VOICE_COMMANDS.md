## 🎤 Tips for Best Results

1. **Speak clearly** - Enunciate your words
2. **Use "Jarvis"** - Say "Jarvis" for more personalized responses
3. **Be specific** - "Open calendar" works better than just "calendar"
4. **Wait for response** - Let Jarvis finish speaking before next command
5. **Check console** - Open browser console (F12) to see what Jarvis heard
